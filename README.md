# Rebepal Madu · B2B Honey Supply · Indonesia

Research & Field Notes

This repository contains the research, operating ideas, experiments, and field notes behind **Rebepal Madu**, an Indonesian B2B honey supplier serving industrial and commercial buyers.

The repository is primarily a knowledge and publishing layer. It is not a software product or open-source application.

## The Lean Local Commerce Model

The main framework documented here is:

**The Lean Local Commerce Model**
*An Asset-Light Framework for Turning Local Supply into Trusted B2B Commerce*

The model explores how fragmented local supply can become more discoverable, credible, and commercially accessible without unnecessary organizational and infrastructure complexity.

It is a working hypothesis derived from real-world operation, not a universal formula.

Read the white paper:

https://rebepalmadu.github.io/

## What We Explore

* Lean B2B commerce
* Local supplier networks
* Digital discovery and procurement
* Information and legal readiness
* Technology as business leverage
* AI-assisted qualification and routing
* Asset-light operations
* Real-world experiments and field observations

## Why This Exists

Rebepal Madu is used as a real-world laboratory for testing these ideas in an operating B2B business.

The objective is simple:

> Build a better system, not a bigger organization.

The framework will continue to evolve as more evidence and experiments emerge.
